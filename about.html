About Kiambu High School

Over five decades of shaping leaders, scholars, and change-makers

"From humble beginnings to a beacon of excellence — Kiambu High School has shaped generations of leaders, scholars, and change-makers."

The idea of Kiambu High School was conceived in 1968 by a group of Kiambu leaders and prominent citizens who recognised the need for a quality boys' secondary school in the district. The school was formally established in 1971, opening its doors with the first cohort of students on its 35-acre campus in Kiambu County. Built under the Open Plan System with Canadian development assistance, the school's distinctive architecture set it apart from the outset.

Initially a mixed school, Kiambu High School was converted to an all-boys institution in 1993. Our journey is a testament to the enduring spirit of our motto — Faith and Work. Over five decades, the school has grown into one of Kenya's respected Extra-County boarding schools, producing graduates who serve with distinction across every field of human endeavour.

Kiambu High School Alumni Foundation

KHSAF — Stronger Together

The Kiambu High School Alumni Foundation (KHSAF) was born in 1976, just five years after the school opened its doors. What started as a simple desire to stay connected has grown into one of Kenya's most active school alumni networks — a brotherhood united by the motto "Faith and Work."

In 2023, the association underwent a significant transformation — rebranding from the Kiambu High School Old Boys Association (KHSOBA) to the Kiambu High School Alumni Foundation (KHSAF), reflecting a forward-looking vision of philanthropy, mentorship, and institutional development.

🎯 Our Vision

To be the leading alumni foundation in Kenya, driving educational excellence and community development through active engagement and philanthropy.

🤝 Our Mission

To connect, support, and empower Kiambu High School alumni while fostering a culture of giving back — mentoring current students, funding infrastructure, and upholding the values of Faith and Work.
