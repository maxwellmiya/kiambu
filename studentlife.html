Boarding Facilities

Our Dormitories

Students are divided into 13 houses, each named after famous mountains

🏠 Senior Dormitories

Modern dormitories for Form 3 and Form 4 students. Each wing has a common room, reading area, and laundry facilities. 24-hour security and dedicated house masters on every floor.

Capacity: 600 Senior Housemaster: Mr. Paul Gathogo
Wi-Fi Enabled Hot Showers Study Hall Sick Bay

🏠 Junior Dormitories

Well-maintained dormitories for Form 1 and Form 2 students. Includes the 200-bed ESP hostel commissioned in 2012 and the newer 92-capacity dormitory completed in 2019.

Capacity: 600 Junior Housemaster: Mr. Daniel Mwangi
Wi-Fi Enabled Hot Showers Study Hall Mentorship Programme
