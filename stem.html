Kenya CBC Senior School Framework

Under the CBC curriculum, STEM students take core subjects (English, Kiswahili, Mathematics) plus pathway-specific subjects. Assessment is competency-based with School-Based Assessment (SBA), practicals, and the Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC) summative assessments.
