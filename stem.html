Biology

Physics

Mathematics

Mathematical modeling, problem-solving strategies, computational methods, data analysis

Kenya CBC Senior School Framework

Under the CBC curriculum, STEM students take core subjects (English, Kiswahili, Mathematics) plus pathway-specific subjects from Pure Sciences and Technical areas. Assessment is competency-based with School-Based Assessment (SBA), practicals, and KNEC summative assessments.
