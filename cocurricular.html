Our Activities

Life Beyond the Classroom

Where passion meets excellence — discover your talent

🏉

Rugby & Football

Regional football champions 2024 and a powerhouse rugby programme. Our athletes regularly compete at county and national levels. Professional coaching and world-class training facilities on our 35-acre campus.

🏆 Regional Champions 2024 🏃 County Athletics
🎵

Music & Choir

5-time national choir champions under the direction of Ms. Christine Karongo. Our music programme covers classical, contemporary, and traditional Kenyan music. Annual concert is a highlight of the school calendar.

🥇 5× National Champions 🎶 Annual Concert
🎭

Drama & Theatre

Regular participants in the Kenya National Drama Festival. Our annual school play showcases exceptional talent and creativity. Award-winning performances since 1985.

🎬 National Drama Festival 🏅 Best Play Awards
🏊

Swimming

Home to a semi-Olympic swimming pool — a first among Kiambu County schools, launched in 2018. Competitive swimming training, inter-school galas, and water safety programmes for all students.

🏊 Semi-Olympic Pool 🏅 Inter-School Galas
🏀

Basketball & Volleyball

Active basketball and volleyball programmes with dedicated courts. Our teams compete in county and regional tournaments, building teamwork, discipline, and physical fitness.

🏀 County Basketball 🏐 Regional Volleyball
⚜️

Scouts & St. John Ambulance

Active Scouting and St. John Ambulance divisions fostering leadership, first aid skills, community service, and outdoor adventure. Building responsible citizens and future leaders.

⚜️ Kenya Scouts 🏥 St. John Ambulance
