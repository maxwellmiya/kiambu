Life Beyond the Classroom

Football & Athletics

Regional football champions 2024. Our athletes regularly compete at county and national levels in track and field events. Professional coaching and world-class training facilities.

🏆 Regional Champions 2024 🏃 County Athletics
🎵

Music & Choir

5-time national choir champions. Our music programme covers classical, contemporary, and traditional Kenyan music. Annual concert is a highlight of the school calendar.

🥇 5× National Champions 🎶 Annual Concert
🎭

Drama & Theatre

Regular participants in the Kenya National Drama Festival. Our annual school play showcases exceptional talent and creativity. Award-winning performances since 1985.

🎬 National Drama Festival 🏅 Best Play Awards
🎤

Debate & Public Speaking

Active debate club with inter-school competitions, Model United Nations participation, and public speaking workshops. Building future leaders and advocates.

🌍 Model United Nations 🎤 Inter-School Debates
🎨

Fine Art & Photography

Annual art exhibitions, photography competitions, and creative workshops. Our students' work has been featured in national youth art galleries.

🖼️ National Art Gallery 📸 Photo Competitions
🔬

Science & Technology Club

Hackathons, robotics competitions, coding workshops, and science fairs. Our STEM club has produced innovations recognized at national and East African levels.

🤖 E.A. Robotics 2nd Place 💡 National Science Fair
