Discover the Difference

Why Kiambu High School?

A tradition of excellence rooted in faith, discipline, and holistic development

🎓

Academic Excellence

Consistently strong KCSE results — 67.4% scored C+ and above in 2025 with a Mean Grade of B−. Seven well-resourced streams delivering quality education.

🏠

Boarding Community

13 boarding houses named after Kenya's mountains foster brotherhood, discipline, and lifelong friendships on our 35-acre campus.

🏊

World-Class Facilities

Semi-Olympic swimming pool, modern science laboratories, ICT centre, expansive sports fields, and a well-stocked library.

⚽

Vibrant Co-Curricular

Regional football champions, national drama qualifiers, competitive swimming, music festivals, STEM clubs, and debate societies.

🤝

Strong Alumni Network

The Kiambu High School Alumni Foundation (KHSAF) connects over 15,000 alumni who mentor students and invest in the school's future.

✝️

Faith & Character

"Faith and Work" — our motto guides every student. Chaplaincy programmes, leadership councils, and mentorship build men of integrity.
